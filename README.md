# Power-BI-Dashboard-Project-2
This is a Power BI end to end Project.
OBJECTIVE : To contribute to the success of a business by utilizing data analysis techniques, specifically focusing on time series analysis, to provide valuable insights and accurate sales forecasting.
DESCRIPTION: 1) Dashboard Creation: Identify the KPls, design an intuitive and visually appealing dashboard, add interactive visualizations and filtering capabilities to allow users to explore the data at various levels of granularity. 2) Data Analysis: Provide valuable insights to business entities regarding the effectiveness of their sales strategies through visualization and charts. 3) Sales Forecasting: Leverage historic data and apply time series analysis to generate sales forecasts for next 15 days. 4) Actionable Insights and Recommendations: End goal is to share valuable insights and actionable information that can drive strategic decision-making anD supermarket's support the supermarket's goals for growth, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
